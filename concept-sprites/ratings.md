# Drawing review and final self-ratings

These scores are my subjective art assessment on the requested 1–10 scale. Following the request for another revision, I used **9/10 as the minimum acceptance threshold** for this cohesive chibi pixel-art collection. This is a self-review, not an independent professional evaluation.

I assessed silhouette and species recognition, state readability, pixel-edge quality, form shading, color consistency, and suitability at the native 128px size. I inspected the entire collection at native size, each character at 2× nearest-neighbor scale, light and dark background reviews, and layered artwork in Aseprite.

The first drawing pass rated 7–8/10 and did not pass as a complete collection. I recreated the set with these revisions:

- Replaced the tired nose/mouth collision with a separate yawn and a raised paw.
- Replaced diagonal face shading with highlights and shadows that follow the head shape.
- Added restrained cheek, fur, muzzle, and scale clusters.
- Added clearer rear-leg contours and paw shading to curled poses.
- Corrected the resting mouse's feet to match its pink feet in standing poses.
- Removed inappropriate exposed mouse teeth from sad, sick, and sleeping expressions.
- Harmonized scarf highlights with each character's accessory palette.

The previous delivery rated 8–8.5/10. For this higher target, I made another substantial drawing pass and two cleanup passes:

- Rebuilt dead as a belly-up pose with raised, curled paws and species-specific spirit silhouettes.
- Added distinct lowered tails for sad, sick, and tired, instead of repeating alert tails.
- Rebuilt happy feet and raised the dog's ears to strengthen the movement of the pose.
- Gave sad characters gathered paws and a slight head tilt.
- Wrapped sick characters in shaded, folded blankets with visible gripping paws.
- Added green cat irises, warm dog irises, lavender mouse irises, and amber dragon irises, with restrained native-pixel highlights.
- Added the cat's brass bell, the dog's bone tag, and the mouse's leaf brooch.
- Refined ear folds, inner ears, the mouse's forehead tuft, dragon horns, wing membranes, and belly highlights.
- Retained a dark external contour while refining the inside of lit contours.
- Quantized head tilts to the main pixel grid to remove uneven native-pixel stair stepping.
- Fixed a clipped mouse ear and paw/face overlap in the dead pose.
- Outlined and repositioned the sleep symbols to remain clear of the ears and readable on light backgrounds.

All 32 final drawings meet my **9/10** acceptance threshold; the strongest examples rate **9.5/10**. No score was raised without further changes to the artwork. The 9.5 ratings favor the clearest combination of expressive pose, distinctive character features, and controlled pixel detail.
